# QuickBooks Online Bank Reconciliation: A Step-by-Step Guide for Small Business Owners

**A technical writing portfolio sample by Brittany Nation, QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor.**

This repository contains a complete end-user guide that teaches a small business owner how to reconcile a bank or credit card account in QuickBooks Online, from connecting the bank feed to finishing a reconciliation with a difference of zero. The audience is a non-accountant: the guide assumes no bookkeeping background, defines its terms in plain language, and sequences the work the way a reader actually performs it, reviewing transactions first, reconciling second, and troubleshooting only when something does not balance. It is written from hands-on practice. Reconciliation is the core monthly service I perform for clients at Deva Gana LLC, and the problems in the troubleshooting table are the ones that actually stop a reconciliation in real books.

## What this sample demonstrates

- **Task-based procedures.** Every workflow is written as numbered steps in the order the reader performs them, one action per step, with the result of the step stated where a reader needs confirmation.
- **Information architecture.** Content is layered by reader intent: a preparation checklist, a guided first run, a diagnostic reference table, and a condensed checklist for repeat use, so beginners and returning readers each get the right depth.
- **Troubleshooting content.** The table in Part 3 is organized by the symptom the reader sees on screen, not by software feature, and each fix is ordered from most to least likely.
- **Plain-language style.** Second person, active voice, consistent terminology, and bold formatting for every interface element, so a reader can follow along with the guide in one hand and the software open in the other.
- **Callouts with a job to do.** Notes carry orientation, tips prevent known mistakes, and warnings are reserved for the two actions that can silently damage a set of books: excluding real transactions and finishing with a nonzero difference.
- **Subject-matter accuracy.** The procedure reflects the real QuickBooks Online workflow, including the bank feed review tabs, the **Reconcile** page under **Accounting**, the accountant-only **Undo** for a full reconciliation, and the **Reconciliation Discrepancy** report and **Audit log** used to trace changes to closed periods.
